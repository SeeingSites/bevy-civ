# Bevy Civ

Cross-platform Civilization-inspired 4X (hex grid) in Rust + Bevy.

Core systems from Civ 2/3 implemented modularly. Goal: exceed FreeCiv. **All major systems complete.**

## Current Features
- Hex map with terrain, yields, improvements, and resources
- Luxury resources give happiness bonuses
- Strategic resource unit gating
- Workers build Farm / Mine / Road / terrain transform
- Cities with production, growth, building effects, and specialists
- Happiness system with civil disorder
- Units with pathfinding, combat, and selection (expanded unit roster)
- Unique units with special abilities
- Tech tree with prerequisites
- Full governments with production/science modifiers, corruption, free unit support, and upkeep
- Corruption based on distance from capital
- Unit upkeep
- Advanced war AI (aggression-based attack/retreat, targets cities, uses A* pathfinding)
- Computer players and barbarians attack units and capture undefended cities; civilizations fight those they are at war with, and units inside a city defend as if on hills
- AI that researches, founds cities, and improves tiles
- Fog of war and diplomacy treaties (Peace / Alliance / War)
- Victory conditions (Domination / Democracy / Space Race / Culture)
- Score system
- Barbarians
- City capture
- Trade routes (gold + science)
- Sprite-based map: textured terrain, animated water, shores, borders, roads, farms and mines
- Animated units, cities that grow visually, fog of war, minimap
- Interface with status bar, unit and city panels, science and foreign advisors, message log
- Wonder effects
- Save / load (expanded)
- Culture and borders (per-turn generation from population + buildings)
- Great Leaders (elite victories → armies)
- **Naval units** (Galley through Battleship + Transport) with cargo load/unload and naval combat
- **Air units** (Fighter, Bomber, Helicopter, Jet Fighter, Stealth Bomber) with fuel, air combat, and bombing damage
- Espionage (steal tech, sabotage, steal gold, incite unrest)
- Economy (inflation, building maintenance, trade gold, government modifiers)
- **Religion** (Polytheism / Monotheism / Philosophy) with happiness bonuses and science modifiers applied per city
- Unit promotions (Veteran, Elite, Combat I/II, Mobility, Siege) — applied in combat + XP awards
- Turn integration (`src/turn.rs`) — helpers to run culture, economy, and promotion-aware combat
- Hotseat multiplayer scaffolding
- Headless game simulator for balance evaluation

## Maps and scenarios
The start menu offers four games; in each you choose which people to lead.

- **New World** — a random continent map shared by five civilizations
- **The Whole Earth** — eleven civilizations in their historical homelands, 4000 BC, on a map of the real world
- **The Rise of Rome** — the Mediterranean in 280 BC: Rome, Carthage, Macedon, Ptolemaic Egypt, the Seleucids and the Gauls
- **The Age of Discovery** — the world in 1492, from Spain and the Ottomans to Ming China, the Aztecs and the Inca

Scenario files live in `assets/scenarios/` and are generated by `node tools/maps/build.cjs` from
Natural Earth coastlines (public domain) and the definitions in `tools/maps/scenarios.cjs`; add a
scenario there, rebuild, and list its JSON in `src/scenario.rs`.

Land units can cross water once their civilization knows **Seafaring**: they board a boat (boarding
and landing end the move) and sail three tiles a turn.

## Resources and trade
Eighteen resources are scattered by terrain:

- **Strategic** (Horses, Iron, Saltpeter, Coal, Oil, Rubber) are required to build things: Horsemen
  and War Chariots need Horses, Legions and Immortals need Iron, Knights need both, Riflemen need
  Saltpeter, Cannons Iron and Saltpeter, Cavalry Horses and Saltpeter, Tanks Oil and Rubber, and a
  Factory needs Coal and Iron. Each stays invisible until the technology that uses it is known
  (Animal Husbandry, Iron Working, Gunpowder, Steam Power, Refining, Replaceable Parts).
- **Luxuries** (Wine, Spices, Silk, Furs, Dyes, Incense, Ivory, Gems): every different one a city has
  access to keeps one more citizen content; a Marketplace makes further luxuries count for more.
- **Bonus** resources (Wheat, Cattle, Fish, Gold) add food, shields or gold to the city whose land
  they lie in, as do Farms and Mines.

A source only counts when it is inside your borders **and connected**: its tile needs a road, and
roads must link it to the city that wants it. Cities with a Harbor are linked to each other across
the sea. Cities check access when you choose production and stop work if they lose it. Computer
players are spared the road building and use whatever lies inside their borders.

A second connected source of a resource is spare and can be sold; other leaders sell their spares.
Deals are made in the foreign advisor, pay gold every turn, run for 20 turns and end at once if war
breaks out or either side loses the source. The trade advisor (**G**) lists what you own, what
still needs a road, what you import and export, and which units you cannot yet train.

## Diplomacy
Open the foreign advisor (**E**) to deal with the leaders you have met. Each has an attitude towards
you that drifts with treaties, trade, shared enemies, crowded borders, your reputation and your
relative strength. You can propose peace or an alliance, sign a trade agreement (+2 gold a turn),
exchange or buy technology, send a gift, demand tribute or declare war - and they can refuse.
Leaders also come to you: they demand tribute when they are stronger, offer peace when a war drags
on, propose technology swaps, ask you to join their wars, and declare war when relations sour.
Breaking a treaty costs reputation with everyone, allies join each other's wars, and the other
powers fight and make peace among themselves.

## Controls
- **Click / tap** a unit or city to select it (click again to cycle what is on the tile)
- **Click a tile** (or **right-click**) to move the selected unit; moving onto an enemy attacks
- **Drag** to pan, **mouse wheel / pinch** to zoom, **WASD / arrows** to pan, click the minimap to jump
- The game selects the next unit that needs orders and moves the camera to it
- **Space** — skip the selected unit and go to the next; when none is left, end the turn
- **N** or the **Next Unit** button — go to the next unit without skipping this one
- **H** — fortify (or put a non-military unit to sleep) so it is no longer asked for orders
- **Enter** or the **End Turn** button — end the turn now
- **B** — settler founds a city
- **F / M / R / X** — worker builds Farm / Mine / Road / clears terrain
- **Q** — science advisor (choose research), **E** — foreign advisor (treaties, resource deals), **G** — trade advisor (resources)
- **T** — trade routes between your cities, **F2** — save
- **Esc** — close a window or deselect

Every order also has a button, so the game is playable with a mouse or touch screen alone.
City production is chosen from the city panel.

## Art
All sprites are original and generated from code: `tools/art/*.cjs` describe each sprite as SVG
and `node tools/art/generate.cjs` rasterises them into the atlases in `assets/sprites/`
(needs Playwright with Chromium). Fonts are Cinzel and Alegreya Sans (SIL Open Font License,
see `assets/fonts/`).

## Simulator
Standalone pure-Rust simulator (no Bevy dependency):

```bash
cd simulate && cargo run -- --games 100 --max-turns 120
```

### CI
GitHub Actions workflow (`.github/workflows/simulate.yml`) runs on push/PR.

## Running (native)
```bash
cargo run
```

## Play in the browser (Cloudflare)
The game compiles to WebAssembly and is served by a static-assets Cloudflare Worker (`wrangler.jsonc`).

One-time tooling: `rustup target add wasm32-unknown-unknown`, a `wasm-bindgen` CLI matching the
version in `Cargo.lock`, and `npm install`.

```bash
npm run build    # builds ./dist (scripts/build-web.sh)
npm run dev      # serve locally at http://localhost:8787
npm run deploy   # build + wrangler deploy (needs `wrangler login` or an API token)
```

`.github/workflows/deploy.yml` builds on every PR and deploys on every push to `main`. It needs two
repository secrets: `CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN` (the "Edit Cloudflare Workers" token template) and
`CLOUDFLARE_ACCOUNT_ID`. The game is then live at `https://bevy-civ.<your-subdomain>.workers.dev`.
In the browser, **S** saves to localStorage.
